Chapter 2: Working with Tensors (page 21)

1. What is the difference between a shape of [5, 1] and a shape of [5]?
They have different ranks, the first one is two dimensional and the second one is one dimensional.

2. Which function would you use to create a tensor filled with random numbers?
tensor = torch.rand(2, 2)

3. If you have a tensor of dtype = torch.int64 and another of dtype = torch.float32, can you add them together directly? Why or why not?
There would be an error and it wouldn't compile as different dtypes can't be multiplied together. 

4. What is the result of torch.ones(2, 2) * 5?
tensor([5.0, 5.0],
       [5.0, 5.0])

5. Why do we prefer float32 over other data types for training neural networks?
This type gives the best balance between precision and speed.
